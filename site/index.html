Official Event Info
What the competition actually involves

Per the rules: up to 2 students, ~50 minutes at the tournament. Eye protection required (goggles + apron/lab coat, skin covered neck-to-wrist-to-toes). Bring 2 Class III calculators, a double-sided 8.5"×11" note sheet each, the Division B Chemistry Lab Equipment List items, plus a pint bag, gallon bag, 500–600 mL container with lid, and ~30cm cheesecloth square — the supervisor will not provide these.

Final Score = Notebook Score (max 18) + Lab Task Score (max 10 each) + Test Score (max 50) − Penalties. The notebook must be impounded before the tournament with 3+ graded experiments per lab task — this is homework that has to happen weeks in advance, not something to do at the last minute.

Test topics: lipids/carbs/proteins in dairy · labeling regulations · esterification & fat bonding · saturated/unsaturated fats · sugar/starch/fat/protein structure · essential nutrients & RDA · cooking chemistry · allergens · preservation & pasteurization · freezing point depression · non-dairy milk · lactose-free milk & lactose intolerance · emulsification · organic dairy · casein & whey · probiotics.
