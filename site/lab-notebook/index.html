🧪 Lab Notebook

3+ real experiments per lab task, done at home before the tournament, recorded on the official Food Science Experiment Form. This is impounded and graded — up to 18 points, and it can't be crammed the week before.

⚠️ Rules Recap

Each experiment = one Notebook entry, and each entry must test exactly one independent variable. You're encouraged to run more than 3 per task, but only the first 3 are graded — so make your first 3 count, and treat any extras as practice/backup.

Follow the event's safety clothing requirements: goggles, apron/lab coat, covered skin, and tied-back long hair. An adult should supervise home trials; do not taste experimental samples. Verify the current manual before competition.
